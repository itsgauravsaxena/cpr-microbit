# Week 5 — Tinkercad first steps (just play!)

**Goal:** get comfortable in Tinkercad. **No pinball part yet** — this week is all about playing so 3D design stops feeling scary. Perfect for first-timers.

**Tinkercad:** https://www.tinkercad.com

## What you'll learn
- The **workplane** (your 3D table) and how to spin the view
- **Drag a shape** on, then **move**, **rotate**, and **resize** it
- The **Hole** tool — turn a shape into a hole to cut into another shape
- **Group** and **ungroup** to join shapes together

## Your first print: a name tag
Everyone makes a little 3D **name tag** to take home as their first print — a flat box with their name on it using the **Text** shape. It's simple, personal, and motivating.

## Session outline (60 min)
- 10 min — what is 3D printing? pass around a printed part
- 35 min — Tinkercad playground: add a box and a cylinder, resize and rotate them, raise one up, make a **hole** and cut it out, then **group**. Now add a **Text** shape with your name on a flat base
- 15 min — save your name tag and share screens

## ✅ Done when
- Every kid has used **move, rotate, resize, the Hole tool, and group** at least once.
- Everyone has made and **saved** a 3D name tag (a flat base + their name).
- Nobody is scared of Tinkercad any more!
