# Weeks 7-8 — Radio Transmitter & Receiver (fun project)

**Goal:** two micro:bits talking to each other over radio, building from basic messaging to a "find the hidden beacon" game.

## Week 7 — Micro Chat
**Official tutorial:** https://makecode.microbit.org/projects/micro-chat

- 5 min recap
- 45 min — Micro Chat tutorial: two boards sending messages to each other, intro to radio groups/channels
- 10 min — pair up and test sending messages across the room

## Week 8 — Heartbeat Beacon
**Official tutorial:** https://microbit.org/projects/make-it-code-it/heartbeat-beacon/

- 5 min recap of radio basics from week 7
- 35 min — Heartbeat Beacon tutorial: one micro:bit broadcasts a low-power "hello" on a radio channel, the other detects it and shows a heart when nearby
- 15 min — turn it into a game: hide the transmitter micro:bit somewhere in the room, kids use the receiver to "hunt" for it (getting warmer as the signal strengthens)
- 5 min — wrap up
