## 📌 What are the pins for?

The gold stripes let the micro:bit talk to the outside world. ⚡

- **P0, P1, P2** — you can **touch** these, or **clip a wire** to them. Great for our pinball targets!
- **3V** — power **plus (+)**.
- **GND** — **ground (−)**, the "other end" of every circuit.

!!! tip "The golden rule of touch 👆"
    To score by touching metal, the metal connects to a **pin (P0/P1/P2)** *and* your hand or the ball reaches **GND**. Touch closes the loop → the pin **fires**! 🔔

Each socket has three little pins:

- 🟡 **S** — signal (the pin, like P0)
- 🔴 **V** — power **+**
- ⚫ **G** — ground **−**

So instead of clipping onto a thin stripe, you just **plug a wire into the socket**. Much easier — and no crocodile clips falling off! 🐊

1. Clip/plug a wire from the **metal target** to **P0** (the 🟡 signal).
2. Make sure the ball or your hand can reach **GND** (⚫).
3. Touch the metal → **P0 fires** → your code adds points! 🎯
