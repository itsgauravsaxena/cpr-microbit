# Week 7 — Design a sensor mount

**Goal:** a simple bracket in Tinkercad to hold a switch or light sensor at a bumper or the ball drain.

## What you'll design
A little **L-shaped bracket** or a **clip** that holds your sensor in place and lines it up so the ball triggers it. Keep it chunky and forgiving.

## Session outline (60 min)
- 10 min — recap last week's bumper design, introduce today's design challenge
- 40 min — design the bracket in Tinkercad (kid-designed, low-precision, snap/glue-fit is fine)
- 10 min — export/queue for printing

## Tips
- Measure your sensor first (roughly) so the slot is big enough
- A hole that's slightly too big is easy to shim; too small won't fit
