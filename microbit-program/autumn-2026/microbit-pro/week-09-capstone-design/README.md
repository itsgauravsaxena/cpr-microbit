# Week 9 — Capstone: design your own gadget

> 💡 Your turn. Invent something — an alarm, a game, an instrument, a step-counting pet — and start building it.

**Goal:** plan and start your own project, reusing the blocks you've learned all term.

## What you’ll build

Whatever you choose! Pick **one sensor** (light, temperature, sound, motion) and **one output** (LEDs, sound, radio) and combine them.

!!! tip "Stuck for an idea?"
    - A **door alarm** — light sensor + loud sound when someone opens the cupboard
    - A **reaction trainer** — how fast are you today? keep a high score
    - A **radio walkie-talkie** with your own secret codes
    - A **pet that needs steps** — walk to keep it happy
    - A **thermometer badge** that changes face with the temperature

## Session outline (60 min)
- 10 min — idea storm on the whiteboard; every idea goes up
- 10 min — pick one, sketch it: what input? what output? what happens?
- 35 min — start building; volunteers help unblock
- 5 min — say out loud what you'll finish next week

## ✅ Done when
- Everyone has a written plan (input → what happens → output).
- Everyone has *something* running, even if it's only half the idea.
