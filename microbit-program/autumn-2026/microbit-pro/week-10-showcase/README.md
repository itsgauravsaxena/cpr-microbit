## What you’ll build

Your own project, finished — with a name, a demo, and the code saved in `code/`.

## Session plan (60 min + 20 min break + 30 min)

### Block 1 — 60 min
- 25 min — finish and debug your project
- 10 min — save your `.hex` / share link into `code/` and give it a name
- 20 min — demos: 2 minutes each, invite the beginners group
- 5 min — certificates and celebrate

### ☕ Break — 20 min
Snacks and running around. Boards stay on the table.

### Block 2 — 30 min · Demos & celebrate
- 15 min — demos: 2 minutes each — what it does, show it working, the hardest part
- 5 min — invite the Beginners group in to play with the gadgets
- 5 min — certificates, group photo, and ideas for next term
- 5 min — 🎉 **Kahoot quiz** — whole group together

## ✅ Done when
- Every project runs on a real micro:bit and has been demoed.
- Every project's code is saved in its `code/` folder.

## How it works

A good demo is 2 minutes: say what it does, show it working, say the hardest part. Practise once before you present — it makes a huge difference.

## Extra challenges
- Write a one-line 'instructions' card so anyone can pick it up and play.
- Record a short video of it working to keep.
- Help someone else finish theirs.

## If it doesn’t work
- Doesn't work on the day? Show the simulator — it still counts!
- Battery dead? Keep a USB cable and a laptop as backup.
